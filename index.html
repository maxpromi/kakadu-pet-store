FAQ

Questions? Kakadu has answers.

Didn’t find yours? Message us on WhatsApp — a real person replies within 15 minutes.

How fast is delivery?

Orders placed before 14:00 ship the same day. Delivery takes 24–48 hours anywhere in Israel, and it’s free from ₪199.

Can I return a product?

Yes — unopened items can be returned within 30 days. Toys your pet didn’t like? Write to us, we’ll find a solution.

Do you help choose food for birds?

Absolutely. Our team works with avian vets and will build a diet plan for your parrot for free.

Is there a physical store?

Our showroom is in Tel Aviv — come meet Kakadu (the real one) on Fridays 10:00–14:00.
